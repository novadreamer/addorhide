The **Hidden / Undo** message after you hide a video now appears in the middle of the browser window instead of the bottom-left corner, at the same compact size as before.

### Install
- **Chrome Web Store:** search for [AddOrHide](https://chromewebstore.google.com/search/AddOrHide).
- **Developer mode:** download `AddOrHide-1.0.13.zip` below, extract it to a folder you keep, open `chrome://extensions` (Brave: `brave://extensions`), turn on **Developer mode**, choose **Load unpacked** and pick that folder. A Developer-mode copy keeps its own data: use **Options → Your data → Export / Import** to move your lists.
