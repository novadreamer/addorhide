AddOrHide 1.1.13 is the version submitted to the Chrome Web Store. It asks for the same permissions as 1.0.13.

**New since 1.0.13**
- **Continue Watching and Watch Later:** videos you play for more than a few seconds resume where you stopped, with a Start over button. Hover over a player to save a video for later.
- **Watched tracking:** a video counts as watched at about 90% of real playback, and progress adds up across sessions. Shorts count towards Watched only.
- **AI-made videos (YouTube):** Labelled as AI hides videos that show AI-made content and keeps tutorials, reviews, news and podcasts about AI.
- **Library pages:** Hidden Videos has tabs, a password lock is available for both pages, and groups can be folded away. Sorting covers size and number of videos.
- **Page awareness:** the popup names the kind of page you are on. On amazon.com, AddOrHide runs on Prime Video pages only.
- **Private windows:** nothing is recorded, so Continue Watching, Watch Later and the Watched switch are hidden there.
- **Fixes:** buttons no longer flicker on Prime Video's hover previews. Episode names and pictures are filled in correctly. There is no Hide channel button on a channel's own page.

**Install from source:** download `AddOrHide-1.1.13.zip`, unzip it, open `chrome://extensions`, turn on Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked.

Full details are in the README under *What's new*.
