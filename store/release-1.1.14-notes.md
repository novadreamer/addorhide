AddOrHide 1.1.14 fixes two problems found after 1.1.13. It asks for the same permissions as 1.0.13.

- **People get real facts, not film genres:** a saved actor or director shows their other jobs, their age (or birth and death years) and their place of birth, taken from the page's own details. Pages about a person no longer give them genres from one of their films. Cards already saved repair themselves once.
- **Genre lists written as links** are read as plain genre names.
- **Tabs stay where you click them:** with the Added or Hidden Videos page open in a normal and a private window at the same time, the normal page no longer jumps from Continue Watching or Watch Later to the first site.

**Install from source:** download `AddOrHide-1.1.14.zip`, unzip it, open `chrome://extensions`, turn on Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked.
