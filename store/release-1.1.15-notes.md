AddOrHide 1.1.15 asks for the same permissions as 1.0.13.

- **No Watched tab in private windows:** the Hidden Videos page now leaves watched videos out of a private window, as Added Videos already does with Continue Watching and Watch Later. There is no Watched tab or section, and Restore all and Remove all neither count nor change watched videos there.

It also includes everything in 1.1.14: real facts for saved people instead of film genres, and tabs that stay where you click them.

**Install from source:** download `AddOrHide-1.1.15.zip`, unzip it, open `chrome://extensions`, turn on Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked.
